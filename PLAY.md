# How to Play FF

FF is an eRepublik-style multiplayer strategy game about building a citizen, growing an economy, helping a country, and competing for national power.

You do not win by finishing one linear campaign. The goal is to become an influential citizen and help your country become stronger through work, production, trade, military action, politics, research, and community activity.

## The main goal

Your long-term goal is to increase your personal power and your country's power.

Personal power comes from:

- gaining experience, levels, strength, achievements, and combat reports;
- keeping enough energy to work, train, recover, and fight;
- building wealth through wages, production, trade, and rewards;
- owning or joining companies;
- contributing to wars, politics, research, media, and social activity.

Country power comes from:

- active citizens who work, train, and fight;
- strong companies and resource logistics;
- useful research bonuses;
- healthy treasury and tax policy;
- controlled regions, resources, and defensive readiness;
- winning battles and campaigns;
- good diplomacy, laws, elections, and public coordination.

## First steps

1. Log in or use the demo account in development.
2. Open **Home** and check the command center.
3. Open **Advisor** if you are not sure what to do next.
4. Complete your daily **Work**, **Train**, and **Daily objectives**.
5. Check **Inventory** and **Recovery Center** before combat.
6. Use **Missions** or **War Room** to fight.
7. Use rewards and resources to grow through factories, market, research, and country systems.

## Core daily loop

The game is built around a repeatable daily loop:

```text
Log in
  -> read Advisor and Notifications
  -> Work for gold and XP
  -> Train for strength and XP
  -> recover or consume supplies for energy
  -> produce, trade, or manage company resources
  -> fight missions or country battles
  -> claim daily objectives and achievements
  -> check country progress, market, politics, and messages
```

Daily actions reset on the game day timer. Missing a daily action means missing progress for that day, so the safest routine is to complete work, training, and claimable rewards first.

## Home and Advisor flow

### Home

Home is the command center. It shows your current citizen state, energy, level, strength, gold, XP progress, daily command queue, daily objectives, achievements, onboarding guidance, hospital status, and shortcuts to the rest of the game.

Use Home when you want a quick answer to:

- Can I work today?
- Can I train today?
- Do I need energy recovery?
- Are daily objective rewards ready?
- Are achievements ready to claim?
- What should I open next?

### Advisor

Advisor is the guided next-step page. It reads your real game state and recommends actions such as:

- claim tutorial rewards;
- claim daily objectives;
- claim achievement medals;
- work before reset;
- train today;
- recover battle energy;
- choose citizenship;
- join active war fronts;
- read notifications;
- clear storage pressure;
- generate a first battle report.

If you are lost, open Advisor first.

## Citizen progression

Your citizen grows through XP, levels, strength, medals, profile stats, and battle history.

Important progression pages:

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Daily state, work, train, recovery, objectives |
| **Advisor** | Adaptive next actions |
| **Training Grounds** | Strength training lanes and training record |
| **Daily Campaign Board** | Rotating objectives and claimable rewards |
| **Medal Cabinet** | Achievements and claimable honors |
| **Citizen Dossier** | Public profile, rank, gear, and combat readiness |
| **Hall of Fame** | Player rankings by profile, strength, and XP |

### Work

Work is a daily economy action. It gives gold and XP and marks your daily work as complete until the next reset.

Use work early because it is reliable income.

### Train

Train is a daily strength action. It increases strength and gives XP. Strength improves combat readiness and long-term citizen power.

Use train every day before reset.

### Training Grounds

Training Grounds expands training into lanes such as basic drill, advanced conditioning, weapon practice, and military academy style progression. Use it to understand training options and recent training history.

### Daily objectives

Daily objectives track real gameplay tasks and become claimable when complete. Claiming them gives rewards and turns normal activity into structured progression.

## Energy and recovery flow

Energy is the action fuel for combat and other demanding activities.

Use this flow before fighting:

1. Check energy on Home.
2. Open **Inventory** to inspect food, weapons, gear, and storage.
3. Use available supplies if energy is low.
4. Open **Recovery Center** if hospital recovery is available.
5. Fight only when you have enough energy and equipment.

### Recovery Center

Recovery Center explains hospital recovery, cooldown, passive regeneration, wallet readiness, and battle-ready thresholds. Use it when energy is low or before a battle session.

## Inventory and equipment flow

Inventory is your supply stash and ledger.

Use it to manage:

- food and consumables;
- weapons and equipment;
- storage capacity;
- equipped gear;
- item durability;
- transaction ledger and recent inventory movement.

Combat, production, market, and recovery all depend on inventory. If an action fails because of missing supplies, check Inventory first.

## Economy, production, and logistics flow

The economy loop is:

```text
Work or earn rewards
  -> collect gold/resources/items
  -> store them in Inventory or company storage
  -> use factories and logistics to produce goods
  -> sell or reserve goods through Market and Contracts
  -> reinvest into upgrades, research, equipment, and war
```

Important economy pages:

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Factories** | Start and claim production jobs, upgrade buildings |
| **Resources & Logistics** | Resource sites, extraction queue, company storage, shipping lanes |
| **Research** | National and company technology trees and bonuses |
| **Market** | Buy, sell, browse order books, and trade goods |
| **Company Contracts** | Reserved trade offers backed by the market service |
| **Labor Exchange** | Company jobs, wages, energy, and production pressure |
| **Companies** | Company directory, company inventory, factories, members, upgrades |

### Factories

Factories turn resources into useful goods. Use factories to produce food, weapons, and other strategic items. Production jobs consume inputs and time, then produce outputs that can feed inventory, market, contracts, or war supply.

### Resources and Logistics

Resources & Logistics shows world resource sites, extraction jobs, company storage, storage pressure, and shipping lanes. Use it to understand where raw materials come from and how they move into usable production.

### Market

Market is where players exchange goods. Use it to buy missing supplies, sell excess production, inspect order books, and participate in the economy.

### Company Contracts

Company Contracts are reserved trade offers. They are useful when a company needs predictable supply or wants to move goods without relying only on the open market.

### Workforce

Labor Exchange lets citizens work for player companies and lets companies expose jobs. It connects citizen energy and wages to company production pressure.

### Companies

Companies organize production at a larger scale. Company owners and members can manage roles, inventory, factories, upgrades, production jobs, and workforce needs.

## Combat and war flow

The combat loop is:

```text
Prepare energy and weapons
  -> choose mission or active battle
  -> fight/contribute
  -> receive combat report
  -> apply rewards, damage, XP, durability, and country contribution
  -> claim related objectives and achievements
```

Important combat pages:

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Missions** | Combat operations, jobs, arsenal, and mission fights |
| **War Room** | Live battles, campaigns, reports, and rewards |
| **Battle Reports** | Contribution console, combat dossier, country impact board |
| **Unit HQ** | Military units, crews, orders, divisions, deployments |
| **Recovery Center** | Energy recovery before or after fighting |
| **Inventory** | Weapons, food, equipment, and durability |

### Missions

Missions are personal combat operations. Use them to fight, earn rewards, consume energy/weapons, and progress combat objectives.

### War Room

War Room is the country battle command page. It shows campaigns, active battles, recent battles, reports, leaderboards, and reward context. Use it when you want to fight for your country.

### Battle Reports

Battle Reports is your combat receipt center. It shows active contribution opportunities, your combat dossier, damage, rewards, equipment wear, score, battle phase snapshots, country impact, and resolved battle history.

### Unit HQ

Military units organize citizens into crews and deployments. Use Unit HQ to browse units, inspect your unit, view orders, and follow unit battle leaderboards.

## World and country flow

The country loop is:

```text
Choose citizenship
  -> understand your country's map, treasury, infrastructure, laws, and diplomacy
  -> contribute through work, taxes, public projects, research, production, politics, and battles
  -> help capture or defend regions
  -> increase country power ranking
```

Important world pages:

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **World** | Countries, regions, citizenship |
| **Country Treasury** | Budget reserves, taxes, recent public ledger changes |
| **Infrastructure Projects** | Citizen-funded public works that unlock national bonuses |
| **Territory** | Region ownership, resources, defense readiness, control history |
| **Country Power Index** | Estimated national strength from live world data |
| **Politics** | Parties, elections, and office holders |
| **Congress** | Laws, proposals, voting, and history |
| **Diplomacy** | Treaties, relations, ratification, rejection, termination |
| **War Room** | Country battles and campaigns |

### Citizenship

Citizenship connects your player to a country. Many country systems make more sense once you have citizenship because your actions can affect national standing.

### Treasury

Country Treasury shows public reserves, taxes, and recent ledger movement. It helps explain how national money is collected and spent.

### Infrastructure Projects

Infrastructure Projects lets citizens contribute gold and materials to public works such as hospital networks, training academies, logistics hubs, fortifications, and research campuses. Completed levels add country bonuses; for example, hospital infrastructure improves energy recovery for citizens.

### Territory

Territory shows region control, resource bonuses, active fronts, regional resources, defense readiness, and control history. Use it to understand why battles matter.

### Country Power Index

Country Power Index estimates national strength from current world data. It gives a strategic overview of which countries are rising or falling.

### Politics and Congress

Politics covers parties, elections, and office holders. Congress covers law proposals, votes, active laws, and history. Use these systems to shape country policy instead of only fighting.

### Diplomacy

Diplomacy manages treaties and country relations. Treaties can be pending, active, rejected, or terminated depending on political decisions.

## Social, media, and notifications flow

The social loop is:

```text
Read notifications
  -> coordinate in chat
  -> inspect citizens and rankings
  -> publish or read newspapers
  -> follow country and company activity
```

Important community pages:

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Notifications Center** | Activity feed, unread alerts, action receipts |
| **Push Notifications** | Push subscription and delivery settings |
| **Inbox** | BBM-style conversations and direct contacts |
| **Newspapers** | Player-generated media and articles |
| **Citizen Dossier** | Public citizen profile |
| **Hall of Fame** | Leaderboards |
| **Medal Cabinet** | Achievements and honors |

### Notifications

Notifications Center is where you check activity receipts, unread alerts, market or battle events, and other game updates. Keep it clear so you do not miss rewards or important state changes.

### Inbox

Inbox is the chat surface for conversations and contacts. Use it to coordinate with other players, companies, and groups.

### Newspapers

Newspapers are the media surface for articles and public information. They support country coordination, propaganda, guides, and community storytelling.

## Account and operations flow

Use **Account Security** for account-level security actions such as sessions and verification flows.

Use **Admin Moderation** only for protected operations, support, moderation, review queues, and economy inspection. It is not normal player gameplay.

## Suggested play styles

You can specialize instead of doing everything equally.

| Play style | Focus |
|---|---|
| Soldier | Train daily, maintain weapons and food, fight in active battles, join units |
| Industrialist | Build factories, manage resources, sell goods, fulfill contracts |
| Trader | Watch market prices, buy low, sell high, manage inventory pressure |
| Politician | Join parties, run in elections, vote on laws, manage treasury priorities |
| Organizer | Run companies, hire workers, coordinate logistics and military supply |
| Journalist | Use newspapers and chat to guide citizens and shape public opinion |
| Completionist | Follow Advisor, complete daily objectives, claim all achievements |

## Best beginner route

1. Open **Advisor**.
2. Complete **Work** and **Train** from Home.
3. Claim any **Daily objectives**.
4. Open **Inventory** and check energy, food, weapons, and storage.
5. Open **Recovery Center** if energy is low.
6. Open **World** and choose or inspect citizenship.
7. Open **Missions** for personal combat or **War Room** for country battles.
8. Review **Battle Reports** after fighting.
9. Use rewards in **Market**, **Factories**, **Research**, or **Training Grounds**.
10. Check **Notifications Center** before logging out.

## What to optimize

Short term:

- Do not miss daily work and training.
- Keep enough energy for planned actions.
- Claim objectives and achievements.
- Avoid full storage.
- Keep combat gear repaired or replaced.

Medium term:

- Build a reliable income source.
- Join or create a company.
- Learn market prices.
- Follow active battles and country needs.
- Pick a play style.

Long term:

- Increase strength, level, wealth, and reputation.
- Help your country win regions and campaigns.
- Support research, treasury, and politics.
- Become visible in rankings, combat reports, media, or office.
